About

Lunar Reserve by Carolina Reserve was founded around a simple idea: create carefully crafted botanical blends with distinctive aromas, flavors, and botanical profiles.

Each blend is crafted from selected botanicals chosen for their aroma, flavor, and traditional use. Lunar Reserve products contain no tobacco or nicotine.

The collection includes Daytime, Relaxation, and Sleep, each crafted with its own distinct botanical profile.

Important: These products are intended for adults age 21 and older. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Smoking any substance, including plant material, exposes the lungs to smoke and may cause respiratory irritation or other health risks. Do not use if pregnant or nursing. Consult a healthcare professional regarding potential interactions with medications or health conditions.
